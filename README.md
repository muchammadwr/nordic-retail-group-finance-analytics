# Finance Analytics Nordic Retail Group

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the financial performance of Nordic Retail Group to identify why strong revenue does not translate into profitability. By examining cost structures, operational efficiency, and scenario risks, the analysis uncovers structural inefficiencies and provides actionable recommendations to improve cost management and achieve sustainable profitability.

## Business Problem

Nordic Retail Group (NRG) operates across the electronics, home and kitchen, and furniture departments. Accurate classification of Direct Costs (COGS) and Operational Expenses is essential for producing reliable Profit and Loss reporting. However, the current reporting process still relies heavily on fragmented and manually managed spreadsheets.

NRG needs to address inconsistent financial data across departments and reporting sources, as well as manual reconciliation processes that increase reporting time and the risk of errors. The absence of a centralized “single version of the truth” limits visibility into departmental profitability, cost performance, and logistics and marketing expenses, ultimately delaying financial and operational decision-making. 

The project is expected to produce a structured and validated financial dataset, an accurate Profit and Loss report with clear classifications of COGS and Operational Expenses, a consolidated balance sheet overview, an operational performance dashboard, and an advanced analytics layer. These deliverables will improve the monitoring of Gross Margin %, Operating Profit, and Budget vs. Actual variance, accelerate reporting, reduce reliance on manual spreadsheets, and identify opportunities to increase profitability
